# JPIA CPALE Study Hub — Starter Library
Mallig Plains Colleges, Inc. | Junior Philippine Institute of Accountants

## Purpose
This starter resource library is designed for six independent Gemini Notebook / NotebookLM notebooks, one per subject, with a Google Drive master folder to maintain source files. It contains folder scaffolding, topic outlines, and reusable content templates. It does **not** claim to contain full CPALE reviewer content, licensed exam questions, or a live Google notebook.

## Suggested master Drive organization
- 00_ADMIN_AND_GUIDELINES
- 01_FAR_Financial_Accounting_and_Reporting
- 02_AFAR_Advanced_Financial_Accounting_and_Reporting
- 03_MAS_Management_Advisory_Services
- 04_AUD_Auditing
- 05_TAX_Taxation
- 06_RFBT_Regulatory_Framework_for_Business_Transactions

## How to publish in Gemini Notebook
1. Sign into notebook.google.com using the Google account intended to **own** the JPIA study hub.
2. Create six notebooks using the subject names above. Label each notebook JPIA CPALE Study Hub | [SUBJECT].
3. In each notebook, add source documents from the corresponding subject folder (PDFs, Google Docs, or other supported files). Notebook sources do not automatically reflect edits to an exported document; verify updates.
4. Add source labels by topic or subtopic, where supported. Keep original source files in Drive as the canonical library.
5. Generate quizzes, flashcards, study guides, and review summaries using the verified source documents.
6. Share notebooks: Owner = main JPIA administrative account; Editor = designated academic/content officers; Viewer = students/members.
7. Keep a master index and audit every update before announcing a reviewer as accurate.

## Content standards
- Prioritize PRC/Board of Accountancy CPALE syllabi and tables of specifications, Philippine laws and issuances, and effective IFRS/PFRS/PSA standards.
- Confirm **current exam coverage** before publication. Exam structure and laws can change.
- Tag each resource with author, source link, publication year, date reviewed, applicable standard/law, and difficulty.
- Publish original explanations or authorized materials only; do not redistribute commercial review-center notes without permission.
- AI-generated explanations and answer keys need faculty/academic-officer review, especially for taxation and regulatory changes.
- Do not upload private student records or confidential personal data.

## Suggested study resource naming convention
`[SUBJECT]_[TOPIC]_[RESOURCE-TYPE]_[YYYY-MM-DD]`
Resource types: CONCEPT, REVIEWER, PROBLEM-SET, ANSWER-KEY, FLASHCARDS, MOCK-EXAM.

## Suggested JPIA governance
- Owner: JPIA administrator (Google account that creates the notebook)
- Lead editors: VP for Academics, subject coordinators, designated faculty reviewer
- Contributors: academic committee / verified members, drafting in Drive
- Viewers: JPIA students and invited reviewees
- Verification: editor reviews accuracy and copyright before publication

**Important:** Gemini Notebook is a source-based notebook service, not an LMS with native hierarchical folder trees. The folder hierarchy lives in Drive; notebook source labels and six separate notebooks make the content navigable.
