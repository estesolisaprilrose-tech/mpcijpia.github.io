# Copy-ready prompts for a source-grounded study hub

## Learn
Using only the selected sources for [SUBJECT] > [TOPIC], explain the concept at a CPALE review level. Cite the precise source supporting each rule. Separate assumptions from verified information.

## Make a reviewer
Prepare a concise but complete reviewer for [TOPIC] with definitions, key rules, illustrations, common traps, and a mini self-check. Cite sources; flag outdated laws or standards.

## Quiz me
Generate 10 original CPALE-style MCQs for [TOPIC] with four answer options each. Put all answers and short explanations at the end. Use only the selected materials and avoid reproducing copyrighted test items verbatim.

## Computational practice
Generate three original numerical problems of increasing difficulty on [TOPIC]. Show full solutions only after the questions, and verify arithmetic and assumptions.

## Identify gaps
Compare my answers to the provided answer key, describe weak competencies, and propose a 7-day remediation plan. Cite the underlying study materials.

## Change check
Identify statements in these sources potentially affected by changes in Philippine law, PRC CPALE coverage, or accounting/auditing standards. Do not assert current legal validity unless a verified, dated authority is among the sources.
